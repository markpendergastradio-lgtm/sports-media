Before you start

  1. There are 80 questions in 5 sections. You answer them one at a time, in order.
  2. Every answer needs a couple of full sentences, at least 12 words. The Next button stays locked until your answer is long enough.
  3. Some questions show diagrams. Pick the correct set-up, then explain why it's right.
  4. Type your own answers. Pasting is switched off.
  5. Your work saves on this computer as you go. When you finish, download your answer sheet and upload it to Showbie.
