The brief

Six-camera league match. The gantry (cameras 1 and 2) is on the main stand side, at the bottom of the plan. Every camera must stay on that side of the 180° line except the two behind the goals.

    Score: 0 / 8

    The 180° line runs along the length of the pitch. Cameras on the far side show the teams attacking the opposite way, so viewers lose track of who is going where.
