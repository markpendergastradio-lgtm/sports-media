Gallery director simulator

You are directing a live match. Watch the four camera previews and cut to the right one at the right moment with the keys 1 to 4 or by clicking a preview. You will be scored on every second of the programme.

← Course site
PROGRAMME
Keys: 1 wide 2 tight 3 player close-up 4 crowd

Director's rules

  • Live play: stay on the wide (1). Go tight (2) only when the ball is in the final third.
  • Goal: be on the wide when the shot goes in.
  • Celebration: the scorer close-up (3), then the crowd (4).
  • Foul or injury: go tight (2) or close-up (3) on the incident.
  • Never go to the crowd while the ball is in play.
  • Hold each shot at least 2 seconds.
